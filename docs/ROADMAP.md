# PeaWork Roadmap

## v0.1 Foundation, delivered
People master, organization, positions, effective-dated hire event, leave workflow, document register, dashboards, reports, API, RBAC basics, audit, SQLite and Docker.

## v0.2 Hire-to-onboard, 6–8 weeks
Requisitions, candidate pipeline, interview scorecards, offers, configurable onboarding journeys, tasks, probation and employee conversion.

## v0.3 Workforce, 6–8 weeks
Attendance, shifts, weekly offs, overtime, timesheets, biometric import/API, leave accrual jobs and manager calendar.

## v0.4 Talent, 8–10 weeks
Goals/OKRs, performance cycles, check-ins, 360 feedback, skills, competencies, development plans and calibration.

## v0.5 India payroll beta, 10–14 weeks
Salary structures, earnings/deductions, attendance inputs, arrears, reimbursements, payroll preview, variance checks, payslips and accounting export. Statutory implementation requires specialist validation and automated golden payroll cases.

## v0.6 Cloud beta, 8–12 weeks
Tenant isolation, PostgreSQL, object storage, subscriptions, custom domains, SSO, SCIM, monitoring, backup/PITR and migration assistant from Local.

## v1.0 production hardening, 6–8 weeks
OWASP ASVS Level 2 verification, penetration test, accessibility, localization, disaster-recovery exercise, load testing to agreed concurrency, data retention and DPDP workflows.

## Later
LMS, compensation planning, benefits, engagement, succession, org modeling, workforce planning, marketplace integrations, mobile/PWA and permission-aware AI assistants.
